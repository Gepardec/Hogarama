Hogarama Community Member [Tharnas](https://github.com/Tharnas/) ported the Habarama client to a very cost efficient
setup using an ESP8266 MicroController.
We are grateful for his contribution and would like to thank him for his effort.

If you are interested in building your own Habarama on a cheaper setup than a raspberry pi check out his documentation
and project [here](https://github.com/Tharnas/habarama-esp8266)
