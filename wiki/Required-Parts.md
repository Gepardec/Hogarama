If you are interested in setting up your own Habarama unit at home, you will need the following parts:

* Raspberry Pi 3 Model B+
* (optional) Raspberry Pi Casing
* Micro USB power supply 2.5A
* microSD card
* Jumper Wire Female to Male 10Pin 20cm
* Jumper Wire Male to Male 10Pin 10cm
* Jumper Wire Female to Female 10Pin 20m
* Breadboard 400 Pins - 8.3 x 5.5 cm
* Sparkfun Soil Moisture Sensor
* Strand wire 1 x 0.75 mm² black 5m
* Strand wire 1 x 0.75 mm² red 5m
* Strand wire 1 x 0.75 mm² yellow 5m
* ADC MCP3008-I/P
* Submersible pump
* Relay
* 6-12V Power Supply for pump
* Water reservoir
* Tube for pump
