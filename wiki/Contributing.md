**First of all: Contributions are welcome!**

To understand why it may take some days until someone is looking at your contributions, here a short description of our
process for external contributions

## Internal Merge Process

Each Friday we gather for a short meeting and look through the new pull requests. Contributions are assigned to one of
the core developers who will do a code review. Depending on the result he or she will merge, ask for changes or reject
the merge request.
